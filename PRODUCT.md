## Platform

web

## Users

Businesses and organizations in Portugal that need a professional website, custom software, or both. They engage MTH Studio to attract and convert customers, improve digital service journeys, or make day-to-day operations easier.

## Product Purpose

MTH Studio is a Portuguese software studio and website design company. It designs and builds websites, quote and booking flows, operations dashboards, CRM automations, client portals, and ongoing website improvements. Success means shipping useful digital work that helps clients win opportunities, reduce administrative friction, and operate more clearly.

## Positioning

MTH Studio combines local-market understanding with website design and operational software in one practice. Clients work with senior practitioners directly, and the work connects customer acquisition with the systems used to deliver the service rather than treating them as separate problems.

## Operating Context

- Based in Leiria, Portugal, with remote projects across Portugal.
- Works Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00, and replies to quote requests within one business day.
- A focused service website usually takes four to six weeks; custom portals and operations tools may take longer.
- Projects follow local discovery, a focused build with working screens shown early, and a measured launch tested on real devices.
- MTH Studio helps shape messaging, content structure, customer journeys, and calls to action alongside design and development.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services include websites, quote and booking flows, operations dashboards, CRM and automation, custom client portals, and ongoing care and optimization.
- Approved technologies and integrations include Next.js, Vercel, Supabase, Stripe, React, HubSpot, webhooks, analytics, calendars, forms, and local SEO.
- Existing tools can be integrated when technically suitable; fit is checked during discovery before an integration is promised.
- Clients own the custom design and code after agreed invoices are paid. Third-party subscriptions remain in the client's business account wherever possible.
- The current quote form validates fields and demonstrates loading and success states locally, but it does not transmit submissions. It must be connected to an approved email service or CRM before publication.
- The current privacy and terms pages are draft prototype copy and require approved legal text before publication.

## Product Principles

- Solve customer acquisition and operational problems as one connected service.
- Make every deliverable useful in the client's real working day.
- Keep project progress visible through early working screens and clear decision points.
- Prefer appropriate, maintainable technology over unnecessary complexity.
- Make claims concrete and support them with real project evidence.
